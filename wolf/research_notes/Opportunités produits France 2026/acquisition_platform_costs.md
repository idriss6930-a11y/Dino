# Paid-acquisition benchmarks and platform/logistics cost structures for a small French Shopify store (Nuitalys), as of November 2026

Research notes for the unit-economics and budget model. Research date: 2026-11-06.

**Method notes (read first):**
- **No full pages were read.** WebFetch could not reach superads.ai or fr.shopify.com because the session's network allowlist blocks them, and per session rules no further hosts were tried. The shared web-search budget then ran out before a final batch of searches. That batch would have covered: Shopify premium/non-EU card rates, how PayPal interacts with Shopify's third-party fee, Klarna's French rate, TikTok Shop–Shopify integration, a France Meta CPM in EUR, Google Shopping CPC detail for Q4 2025, FEVAD returns/mobile figures, and email's share of revenue. Every figure below therefore comes from search-engine summaries of the cited pages.
- **† marks an uncertain page attribution.** For these figures, the search summary did not say which of several co-listed pages carried the number. The cited page is the most likely one.
- **Currency.** USD figures are kept in USD. Euro equivalents use an assumed €0.87 per $1 (about $1.15 per €). This rate is an assumption, not a sourced figure.
- **EST** marks my own estimate. The reasoning is always given.

## 1. Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads in France: CPM, CTR, CPC, conversion, CPA, ROAS and seasonality

### Takeaway
France is a cheap Meta market. In 2025–26 the median CPM across all objectives was about $8–10 and the median CPC about $0.53, roughly half the global level. E-commerce purchase benchmarks cluster around a CPA of $38–48 (about €33–42), with a median ROAS of about 1.9–2.25 for Home & Garden. Q4 is much more expensive:
- CPMs climb from mid-November.
- They peak in Black Friday week, +59% vs September for US brands overall and +116% for home & decor.
- They stay high through December.
- They then fall about 30–40% into January–February.

### Cited Findings
**France-specific (Superads medians, all industries and objectives, USD)**

- A second Superads window (Sep 2025–Aug 2026) gives about $8.06 for France vs about $20.59 globally. The monthly low was $4.69 (Feb 2026) and the high $21.74 (Jul 2026). - France median CPC averaged about $0.53 (Jun 2025–Jun 2026). It went from $0.47 (Jun 2025) to $0.84 (Jun 2026), with a low of $0.42 in Jan 2026. The global figure is about $1.07 — [Superads France CPC](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpc-cost-per-click/france)

**Seasonality**
- US brands in 2025 (Lebesgue) — [Lebesgue BF CPM/ROAS data](https://lebesgue.io/?p=15192):
  - Median CPM was +15% two weeks before Black Friday and +24% the week before.
  - In BF week it was +59% vs September; on BF day itself, +84%.
  - In BF week by category: home & decor +116%, electronics & sporting goods +76%, fashion & footwear +66%.
  - Lebesgue notes that CPM alone does not decide profitability; CPA and margin do.
- The market-wide average CPM fell from about $9.43 (BF weekend) to about $6.63 (early January). The peak was in November, with a mid-December dip — [Lebesgue](https://lebesgue.io/?p=5737)
- Global Meta CPM went from a Nov 2025 peak of $25.22 to a Jan 2026 low of $15.74 — [Clouted](https://clouted.com/blog/meta-advertising-CPM-inflation-statistics). The same publisher elsewhere gives a 30–70% Q4 spike — [Stackmatix seasonal strategy](https://stackmatix.com/blog/meta-ads-seasonal-strategy)

- France: one source says CPMs rise 40–80% in Nov–Dec (Black Friday, Christmas) and 20–30% in January (winter sales) — [Beezy](https://blog.iambeezy.app/fr/cpm-facebook-instagram-france-2026-couts-optimisation/). This is a vendor and its method is undisclosed. Superads France data contradict the January claim: January 2026 was the CPC low and February 2026 the CPM low — [Superads France CPC](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpc-cost-per-click/france); [Superads France CPM](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm/france)

### Inferences
- **Baseline CPM (outside Q4) for a purchase-optimised prospecting campaign in France: EST €7–15, mid about €10.**
  - Superads' all-objective medians ($8–10, about €7–8.4) include cheap awareness and traffic buying, so conversion campaigns sit above them.
  - Global e-commerce CPM ($10.80–17.94), discounted by France's 50–60% gap, gives about €5–9.
  - French agency quotes (€12–20) are at the high end.
- **Funnel check.** CPC = CPM ÷ (link CTR × 1,000). At a €10 CPM and 1.3% link CTR, a link click costs €0.77. At 1.4% landing-page conversion, CPA is about €55.
  - Published medians are lower (about €33–42) because established advertisers' totals include retargeting and brand demand.
  - A new store's cold prospecting is more likely to land around €45–60 at first.
- **Link CTR.** The Superads 2.02% e-commerce CTR is probably all-clicks; link CTR is usually lower. EST link CTR: 0.9–2.0%, mid 1.3%.
- **ROAS.** At AOV €70 and CPA €45, ROAS is about 1.56, below the Home & Garden median of 2.25. The margin structure (§5, §10) matters more than the benchmark.
- **Black Friday 2026 (27 Nov) calendar:**
  - about 13 Nov: +15%
  - about 20 Nov: +25%
  - 23–30 Nov (BF week through Cyber Monday): about +60% overall, and +100% or more for home & decor
  - December: elevated, with a mid-month dip
  - late December to February: trough

  Home/bedding has one of the steepest BF spikes. Prospecting in BF week therefore needs a real conversion lift (for example the duo offer) to stay profitable.
- **VAT under the franchise.** For a micro-entrepreneur under the VAT franchise, the CPA the platform reports understates true cost by the non-recoverable VAT on ad spend (see §5). Effective CPA ≈ reported CPA × 1.20.

### Gaps
- **No French vertical benchmarks.** None were retrievable for home/bedding, health & wellness or pets on Meta. Superads has France vertical pages (e.g., wellness), but fetching was blocked.
- **No consistent French monthly CPM series** for Oct–Jan. The Superads monthly values swing a lot (e.g., $21.74 in July 2026), which suggests small samples.
- **Not found:** the definitions behind link CTR vs all-clicks CTR in Superads, and a French landing-page-to-purchase conversion rate for Meta traffic.

## 2. TikTok Ads France and TikTok Shop France

### Takeaway
Sources report TikTok ads in France as cheaper per impression than Meta: CPM about €3–10 and CPC about €0.20–0.80, against global 2025 CPMs of $8–13 and CTRs of 0.9–1.8%. No France conversion or CPA benchmarks were found.

TikTok Shop France's commission went from 5% to 9% on 8 January 2026. Some electronics categories stay at 7%. A 4%, 60-day new-seller rate is reported but not confirmed. There is no subscription fee. Creator affiliate commissions are set by the seller, typically 10–20% (range 5–30%). A business registered in France, including a micro-entreprise, is reportedly eligible to open a shop.

### Cited Findings
**TikTok Ads**
- A French agency page puts TikTok CPM in France at about €3–8, 40–60% cheaper than Meta (€12–20). Its comparison table shows a CPM of €3.80 and a CPC of €0.20–0.80 — [Webtonic (Lyon)](https://www.webtonic.io/locations/lyon-tiktok-ads)†
- A European e-commerce guide gives a CPM of about €5–10 and a CPC of €0.30–0.80, "2–3x cheaper than an equivalent Meta campaign" — [Fullmetrix](https://fullmetrix.com/en/blog/tiktok-ads-ecommerce-guide)†
- Influee 2026 benchmarks (2025 data, global): CPM $13.26 (up 16% from $11.43), CTR 1.77% (+13.74%) — [Influee TikTok benchmarks (FR)](https://influee.co/fr/blog/tiktok-ads-benchmarks)
- Another source gives a median CPM of $8.00, a CPC of $0.80 and an In-Feed CTR of 0.9% — [ttcalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/data/earnings/tiktok-advertising-rates-benchmarks/)†

**TikTok Shop: seller fees**
- The standard commission went from 5% to 9% in January 2026 in France, Germany, Spain, Italy and Ireland — [Marketing4eCommerce](https://marketing4ecommerce.net/en/tiktok-shop-increases-commission/); [Ecommerce Nation](https://www.ecommerce-nation.fr/tiktok-shop-les-commissions-doublent-janvier-2026/)
- Consumer electronics and electronic beauty stay at 7%. A July 2026 guide says the commission is calculated excluding VAT — [Margeo](https://margeoapp.com/blog/tiktok-shop-europe-expansion-2026-revendeur). Another source contradicts this, saying 9% is VAT-inclusive per TikTok Shop Academy — [Speed-Ecom](https://speed-ecom.eu/tiktok-shop-debarque-en-france-tout-ce-que-vous-devez-savoir-avant-de-vous-lancer/)
- New sellers reportedly pay 4% for 60 days — [Marketing4eCommerce](https://marketing4ecommerce.net/en/tiktok-shop-increases-commission/); [Swello](https://swello.com/fr/blog/?p=21438). eDesk cautions that reductions apply only to some categories or short periods, and advises checking the rate in Seller Center — [eDesk](https://www.edesk.com/fr/blog/combien-coute-la-vente-sur-tiktok-shop-fr/)
- There is no registration or subscription fee, only commission on each sale — [Swello](https://swello.com/fr/blog/?p=21438)

**TikTok Shop: affiliates and eligibility**
- Affiliate commissions are set by the seller, not the platform. French guides cite 5–20%, often 10–20%, and 5–30% depending on category — [Formalive](https://www.formalive.fr/affiliation-tiktok-shop/)†; [JDN](https://www.journaldunet.com/retail/1537961-tout-ce-qu-il-faut-savoir-sur-tiktok-shop/)†
- A bookkeeping guide reports rates from nearly 0% to about 50%, with a health-category median of about 20% — [Beancount](https://beancount.io/fr/blog/2026/07/25/tiktok-shop-seller-bookkeeping-fees-commissions-guide)
- If the customer returns the product, the creator's commission is cancelled — [Formalive](https://www.formalive.fr/affiliation-tiktok-shop/)†
- To open a shop, a French seller guide says you need a legal structure registered in France (micro-entreprise, SASU, EURL or other) — [Digitiz](https://digitiz.fr/se-lancer-tiktok-shop/)†; [Swello](https://swello.com/fr/blog/?p=21438)†
- Creator-side requirements: 18+ and a TikTok Pro account. Follower minimums are disputed (1,000 vs none) — [Formalive](https://www.formalive.fr/affiliation-tiktok-shop/)†

### Inferences
- **Effective TikTok Shop take rate for a creator-driven sale.** The platform's 9% plus a 10–20% affiliate commission comes to 19–29% of GMV, before shipping. At €59: commission €5.31, a 15% affiliate €8.85, total about €14.16 per unit.
- **Possible VAT on TikTok's commission.** If VAT is charged or must be self-assessed, a franchise seller cannot recover it. That would add about 1.8 points (9% × 20%) — EST.
- **TikTok Ads modelling.** The cheaper CPM does not mean a cheaper CPA. With no France conversion data, model TikTok traffic with a lower landing-page conversion than Meta — EST 0.5–1.5%.

### Gaps
- **Not researched (search budget exhausted):**
  - whether the Shopify ↔ TikTok Shop integration (TikTok sales-channel app) supports French TikTok Shop
  - payment-processing or settlement fees on TikTok Shop France
  - fulfilment and SLA rules, which may conflict with AliExpress/DSers dropshipping
- **No France-specific TikTok Ads conversion rate or CPA** was found.

## 3. Google Ads France: CPCs for purchase-intent queries, Shopping/Performance Max, free listings and CSS

### Takeaway
- **No published Google CPCs were found** for "oreiller ergonomique", "oreiller cervical" or "oreiller mémoire de forme". Use Keyword Planner on the live account.
- **Shopping CPC benchmarks:** about €0.29–0.30 across European e-commerce (smarter-ecommerce, Q3 2025, all categories), and about $0.66 in the US. French CPCs across all campaign types run €0.50–3.00.
- **Free product listings** are available, through any comparison shopping service (CSS) in Europe.
- **Independent CSSs** claim up to about 20% lower Shopping CPCs than Google Shopping Europe (GSE). This is vendor marketing. The saving applies only to Shopping inventory, including Shopping placements inside Performance Max.

### Cited Findings
- European e-commerce Shopping CPC was relatively stable around €0.29–0.30 (Q3 2025) — [smarter-ecommerce CPC metric](https://smarter-ecommerce.com/en/smec-market-observer/metrics/cpc). Shopping CPC inflation slowed from about 16% to about 7% over four quarters, while Search CPC growth stayed flat — [smarter-ecommerce Q4 2025 report](https://smarter-ecommerce.com/en/smec-market-observer/reports/Q4_2025/)
- The average CPC in France in 2025 was €0.50–3.00 across all campaign types — [Copeps](https://copeps.fr/actualites/adwords-clic-comptabilise/)
- Average Shopping CPC was $0.66 (Store Growers, 2025) — [Thestacc](https://thestacc.com/fr/blog/ppc-statistics/)
- Average e-commerce Search CPC was $1.16 (DemandSage, 2025) — [Lionelz](https://lionelz.com/fr/blog/google-ads-ecommerce/)
- **How CSSs work.** GSE is the default CSS. France is in the CSS programme. A CSS typically takes a fixed percentage margin on the CPC. Competing CSSs say GSE keeps about 20% and that they don't, giving about 20% lower CPCs — [Cobiro](https://resources.cobiro.com/google-shopping-css/cpc-advantage/); [Adsmurai](https://www.adsmurai.com/en/articles/google-css-reduce-cpc-google-shopping); [Bidnamic](https://www.bidnamic.com/resources/the-effect-of-css-account-to-overall-performance-traffic)
- Another provider says GSE has confirmed it keeps part of every CPC but has not published the share. The CSS advantage applies only to Shopping placements within Performance Max, so the blended saving is smaller when PMax spends on Search, Display or YouTube — [Adstrong](https://adstrong.com/?p=10958)†
- **Official rule on free listings.** In Europe, products can go into free listings through any CSS, but only one CSS can be selected for free listings — [Google Merchant Center Help](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12652686)
- CSS-partner apps exist on the Shopify App Store (no pricing captured) — [Shopify App Store: AR Google CSS Program Partner](https://apps.shopify.com/google-css-shopxyz-comparison-shopping-partner?locale=fr)
- **Keyword volume.** The only figure found was about 1,100 monthly Amazon searches for "oreiller cervical", on what is probably MerchantWords' Canadian dataset. It is not Google and not France — [MerchantWords](https://www.merchantwords.com/search/ca/cervical)
- **Who appears in search for these queries** (from the results seen during research):
  - comparison/review publishers — [Futura-Sciences oreiller cervical](https://www.futura-sciences.com/maison/comparatifs/meilleur-oreiller-cervical-comparatif/), [Futura-Sciences oreiller ergonomique](https://www.futura-sciences.com/maison/comparatifs/meilleur-oreiller-ergonomique-comparatif/)
  - marketplaces and retailers — [Rue du Commerce](https://www.rueducommerce.fr/p/m24072216175.html), [Boulanger](https://www.boulanger.com/ref/9000971063), [Redcare Pharmacie (Tempur)](https://www.redcare-pharmacie.fr/hygiene-et-sante/BE01615673/tempur-oreiller-cervical-original-50-x-31-x-11-5-8-5-cm.htm)
  - deal sites — [Dealabs (Bedsure)](https://www.dealabs.com/bons-plans/oreiller-cervical-bedsure-66x38-cm-memoire-de-forme-double-coeur-3-zones-de-soutien-oeko-tex-3434685)

### Inferences
- **EST CPCs for French bedding purchase-intent queries:**
  - Shopping: €0.30–1.00, mid about €0.55. The anchor is the €0.29–0.30 European average and the US $0.66 (about €0.57), adjusted upward for a contested category.
  - Search: €0.50–2.00, mid about €0.90. The anchor is France's €0.50–3.00 all-campaign range and the US $1.16 (about €1.01).

- **CSS saving.** It only matters once Shopping spend is material. EST realised saving: 0–20%, mid about 10%, net of the CSS provider's own fee.

## 5. Shopify France 2026: plan price, Shopify Payments, third-party fee, PayPal, Klarna, chargebacks (plus VAT for a franchise micro-entrepreneur)

### Takeaway
- **Basic plan:** €36/month billed monthly, or €27/month billed annually. Whether these prices include VAT is unclear; one source lists €32/€24 excluding VAT.
- **Shopify Payments on Basic:** 1.5% + €0.25 per standard EU card online.
- **PayPal France:** 2.90% + €0.35.
- **Klarna** runs inside Shopify Payments, but no official French rate was found.
- **Chargebacks:** €15 each, and in France the fee is not refunded even if the merchant wins.
- **VAT cost:** a micro-entrepreneur under the VAT franchise cannot recover VAT. Shopify's processing fees carry 0% VAT. Ad spend and SaaS bought from foreign/Irish providers effectively carry 20% VAT that becomes a pure cost.

## 7. Dropshipping logistics with EU stock, China-direct delivery and the July 2026 EU customs duty change

### Takeaway
The current setup, AliExpress with a supplier warehouse in France delivering in 3–7 days, avoids the 2026 import charges at order level.

China-direct shipping to France typically takes about 1–3 weeks (7–25 days by service), and it now carries extra charges:
- since 1 July 2026, an EU flat customs duty of €3 per item category on parcels under €150, which replaced France's €2-per-item parcel tax that ran from 1 March to 30 June 2026
- per economie.gouv.fr, a further €2 handling fee per item category from 1 November 2026

EU-stock alternatives:
- CJ Dropshipping local warehouses: 2–6 days
- BigBuy (Spain): about €120–150/month subscription plus fees
- Spocket: $39.99–99.99/month
- Syncee: $29–99/month

### Inferences
- **Abandoned-cart flow.** At about 70% cart abandonment (§4) and a 3.33% placed-order rate per flow recipient, a basic flow recovers roughly 2–3% of abandoned checkouts that have a captured email. Vendor claims of "10–15% recovery" use different definitions.
- **Repeat purchase for a single-SKU durable pillow, EST 4–12% a year, mid about 8%.** That is below the home-goods range (12–26%) because there is no consumable or companion product. A second SKU such as a pillowcase, a travel pillow or a pet version (if the catalogue expands) would raise it.
- **Email/SMS share of total revenue, EST 5–20%, mid about 12%,** for a young single-product store relying on paid acquisition. This is not sourced; see Gaps.

### Inferences
- **Core formulas:**
  - CPC = CPM ÷ (CTR × 1,000)
  - CPA = CPC ÷ conversion rate
  - effective CPA = CPA × (1 + V1)
  - contribution per order = AOV − landed COGS (incl. non-recoverable VAT) − AOV × P7 − AOV × R1 (plus unrecovered goods cost on refunds) − AOV × R2 (plus the €15 fee) − R3 × R4 (or operator time) − fixed costs per order (P1 + A6 ÷ monthly orders)
  - break-even CPA = contribution per order before ads
  - break-even ROAS = AOV ÷ break-even CPA
- **Worked example with a hypothetical COGS** (supplier cost was not part of this research):
  - With AOV €70 and a hypothetical landed COGS of €20: payment ≈ €1.7, refunds/chargebacks ≈ €4.5, CS ≈ €0.4.
  - That leaves about €43 before ads and fixed costs.
  - The mid effective CPA is €45 × 1.2 = €54, which is above €43.
  - Break-even then needs a cheaper CPA (better creative or offer), a higher AOV (more duo sales) or a lower COGS.
- **Q4 2026 budget.** Apply S1–S6 to M1 for each period. Since prospecting in BF week costs about 1.6–2.2x the baseline CPM, it only makes sense if conversion rises proportionally. In practice that means a strong offer and a retargeting-heavy mix. Budget can then shift to January–February, when CPMs are about 15–35% below baseline.
- **Staying with EU stock.** It avoids L4 (€3–5 per parcel since 1 Jul 2026, and since 1 Nov 2026 per economie.gouv.fr) and the extra support and refund load that comes with 2–3-week delivery.

### Gaps
- **Estimate rows to replace with real data first.** These rows (EST, with no or weak direct sourcing) carry the most model risk: M1, M2, M4, T4, G1, G2, P3, P4, P6, R1, R6, E4, E5. The best substitutes are the store's own data:
  - Meta and Google Ads account data (Keyword Planner for G2)
  - Shopify billing (P1, P4)
  - Shopify Payments payout reports (P6, P7)
  - order and refund history (R1, R2)
