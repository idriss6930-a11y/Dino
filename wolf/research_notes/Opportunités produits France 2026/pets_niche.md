## 1. French pet market size, pet population and online share

### Takeaway
The FACCO/Odoxa 2025-26 barometer counts about 73.4 M companion animals in France: 16.6 M cats and about 9.6–9.7 M dogs. Cats now clearly lead and dog numbers are falling. Promojardin-Promanimal puts the pet-products market at about €6.7 bn in 2025, with growth slowing to about +1%. E-commerce is a stable ~15% of pet-product purchases (2024 figure). I found no online share for accessories alone.

### Cited Findings
**Pet population (FACCO/Odoxa 2025-2026, published late May/June 2026)**
- Total of 73.4 M companion animals. Survey ran June 2025–Feb 2026 on 10,098 people representative of French adults aged 18+. Sources: [L'Essentiel, 10 Jun 2026](https://www.lessentiel.fr/animaux/2026-06-10/la-france-compte-734-millions-danimaux-de-compagnie-22612); [Le Point Vétérinaire 2026](https://www.lepointveterinaire.fr/veterinaire/planete-vet/animal-societe/possession-suivi-veterinaire-alimentation-dit-barometre-faccoodoxa-2026-15hngc3)
- 16.6 M cats. 2026 coverage gives 9.6 M dogs, while one summary gives 9.7 M (**conflict**). Sources: [DirectMag, "En 2026, le chat détrône le chien"](https://www.directmag.com/chien-chat-animaux/info19305/chat-premier-animal-compagnie-france-2026.html); [L'Essentiel](https://www.lessentiel.fr/animaux/2026-06-10/la-france-compte-734-millions-danimaux-de-compagnie-22612). The 9.7 M figure (labelled "FACCO/Kantar 2025") is in [Planète Grandes Écoles](https://www.planetegrandesecoles.com/?p=127526), which also calls France Europe's largest pet market.
- Fish are the largest group (29.1 M). "Six Français sur dix" share their home with at least one animal. Sources: [L'Essentiel](https://www.lessentiel.fr/animaux/2026-06-10/la-france-compte-734-millions-danimaux-de-compagnie-22612); [Wakaorbit analysis](https://fideles-compagnons.wakaorbit.com/73-4-millions-d-animaux-de-compagnie-en-france-ce-que-ce-chiffre-dit)

The trend headline "Moins de chiens, plus de chats" is from [Dépêche Vétérinaire](https://www.depecheveterinaire.com/moins-de-chiens-plus-de-chats%C2%A0-la-tendance-se-confirme-selon-la-facco_67994D7EB768A566.html).

- **Conflicting sizes:** a Xerfi figure of €14 bn, with petfood alone at €6.3 bn in 2025, is cited by [Toute la Franchise](https://www.toute-la-franchise.com/vie-de-la-franchise-A31733-marche-animaux-compagnie.html). **Online share**

- Zooplus was reported as France's most-visited online pet store. The article is undated and likely older than 2023: [LSA](https://www.lsa-conso.fr/zooplus-demeure-l-animalerie-en-ligne-la-plus-populaire-en-france,355933?facet1=pdf)

**Trend signals (low-confidence marketing blog)**
- Google interest for cat accessories peaked in Sep (47), Oct (59) and Dec 2025 (67, index values). Source: [Comdewouf, updated May 2026](https://comdewouf.fr/marche-animaux-de-compagnie-etude-tendances)

### Inferences
- There are about 1.7 cats per dog, and the dog population is shrinking. Cat products (fountains, brushes, rollers) therefore have a larger and growing installed base than dog-only products (car covers, paw cleaners, most lick mats).
- With the market growing about +1% in value in 2025, a new store must take share from others; it won't ride market growth. The winning levers are angle, bundle and price.
- 15% online × €6.7 bn ≈ €1.0 bn of online pet-product spend, petfood included. This is my calculation and assumes the 2024 share held in 2025. Accessories probably over-index online compared with petfood bought in supermarkets, but that is speculative.
- Seasonality: the Sep–Dec interest peak plus Christmas gifting makes Nov–Dec 2026 a reasonable launch window for gifts and cat items. Muddy-weather products (paw cleaner, car cover) also fit Nov–Feb.

### Gaps
- No accessories-only market size or online share found (Promojardin "Panorama" detail not in the excerpts).
- The FACCO 2026 PDF itself was not accessible (facco.fr blocked). Figures come from press coverage.
- No split of online pet sales between Amazon, zooplus and others.

## 2. Cross-cutting factors: customs, VAT and regulation for China-direct vs EU-stock dropshipping (H2 2026)

### Takeaway
Since 1 July 2026, each low-value parcel from outside the EU pays a flat €3 EU customs duty per item category, on top of import VAT. An EU handling fee is due by 1 Nov 2026 at the latest. France's own €2-per-item parcel tax (from 1 Mar 2026) was suspended in favour of the EU duty. Every product needs an EU-based responsible operator under GPSR. Electrical and battery products (fountains) add REP registrations for DEEE and batteries, each with its own IDU.

### Cited Findings
**Customs and parcel taxes**
- Regulation (EU) 2026/382 ends the duty exemption for consignments under €150. As a transitional measure (1 Jul 2026 → 1 Jul 2028) it sets a flat €3 duty per item, and VAT stays due on top. Sources: [Lexis Veille](https://lexisveille.fr/petits-colis-importes-entree-en-vigueur-au-1er-juillet-du-droit-de-douane-europeen-et-suspension-de); [Lexgo](https://www.lexgo.be/fr/actualites-et-articles/14980-la-fin-de-l-exemption-douaniere-de-150-l-union-europeenne-instaure-un-droit-forfaitaire-de-3-sur-les-petits-colis); [Le Monde du Droit](https://www.lemondedudroit.fr/fiscal/309-fiscalite-des-entreprises/103092-ue-nouvelles-regles-en-matiere-de-droits-de-douane-pour-les-petits-colis.html)
- **The unit is ambiguous.** The Commission (via Belgium) says the duty applies per item based on the tariff category: [news.belgium.be](https://news.belgium.be/fr/node/44310/pdf). Luxembourg says "3 euros par type d'article": [Gouvernement.lu](https://gouvernement.lu/dam-assets/images-documents/actualites/2026/06-juin/10-douane-e-commerce/communiqu-de-presse.pdf). Option Finance says €3 per declared tariff position "indépendamment de la quantité": [Option Finance](https://www.optionfinance.fr/entreprise-expertise/importation-de-petits-colis-dans-lunion-europeenne-ce-qui-change-pour-les-operateurs.html). See also [Ecommerce Nation](https://www.ecommerce-nation.fr/taxe-petits-colis-ue-3-euros-categorie-juillet-2026/).
- French "taxe sur les petits colis": Art. 82 of the 2026 finance law (loi n° 2026-103, 19 Feb 2026). It has applied since 1 Mar 2026 at €2 per article on non-EU parcels worth ≤€150, and was meant as a stopgap until an EU handling-fee scheme (expected Nov 2026). Sources: [info.gouv.fr](https://www.info.gouv.fr/actualite/taxe-sur-les-petits-colis-comment-ca-marche); [economie.gouv.fr](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/particuliers/tout-savoir-sur-la-taxe-sur-les-petits-colis); [CCI Lyon Métropole](https://www.lyon-metropole.cci.fr/actualite/taxe-2-euros-sur-les-petits-colis-entree-en-vigueur-mars-2026)
- France suspended the €2 levy in favour of the EU €3 duty from 1 Jul 2026, and an EU handling fee is to be in place by 1 Nov 2026 at the latest. Sources: [Legifiscal](https://www.legifiscal.fr/actualites-fiscales/4550-taxe-petits-colis-france-suspend-prelevement-2-profit-nouveau-droit-douane-europeen.html); [Lexis Veille](https://lexisveille.fr/petits-colis-importes-entree-en-vigueur-au-1er-juillet-du-droit-de-douane-europeen-et-suspension-de); DGDDI/DGFiP webinar: [douane.gouv.fr (PDF)](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2026-02/18/Support_Webinaire-DGDDI-DGFIP-TPCv12022026vdef.pdf). **Not confirmed:** whether the handling fee is live as of 6 Nov 2026, and its amount.

**REP (extended producer responsibility)**
- Dropshipping does not exempt a seller from REP. Don't assume a distant supplier has met your French obligations. A seller that isn't the REP "producer" must get the producer's identifiers from its supplier. Sources: [Le Freelance](https://lefreelance.fr/articles/rep-freelance-idu-eco-organisme-produits/); [Eldris DEEE/dropshipping guide](https://epr.eldris.ai/fr/centre-de-donnees/guides-enregistrement-rep/conformite-deee-dropshipping-logistique-2025/); DREETS dropshipping guidance: [DREETS Hauts-de-France (PDF)](https://hauts-de-france.dreets.gouv.fr/sites/hauts-de-france.dreets.gouv.fr/IMG/pdf/dropshipping.pdf)
- There is one IDU (identifiant unique, 15 characters starting with "FR") per REP stream: for example DEEE, DEA (furniture) and Piles (batteries), plus packaging and paper when products ship in packaging with a leaflet. Marketplaces must collect it (Code de l'environnement R541-167). Sources: [Fnac Darty Marketplace](https://marketplace.fnacdarty.com/s/article/Comment-renseigner-mes-IDU-éco-participation-dans-mon-compte-vendeur); [Rakuten legal page](https://global.fr.shopping.rakuten.com/en/documentation-categorie/legal-informations); [GHR IDU memo (PDF)](https://www.ghr.fr/IMG/pdf/fiche_memo_-_idu_fr.pdf)
- Failing to declare or join a scheme can bring an administrative fine of up to €30,000. Source: [Le Freelance](https://lefreelance.fr/articles/rep-freelance-idu-eco-organisme-produits/) (secondary)

**GPSR**
- Regulation (EU) 2023/988 has applied since 13 Dec 2024. Under Art. 16, an EU-established economic operator (manufacturer, importer, authorised representative or fulfilment provider) must be responsible for each product. Selling under your own brand makes you the "manufacturer" (Art. 13). Source: [donneespersonnelles.fr](https://donneespersonnelles.fr/gpsr)
- A logistics warehouse is not the responsible person by default: [GetTransport](https://blog.gettransport.com/fr/logistics-guide/gpsr-eu-product-safety-2026-non-eu-seller-guide/). Marketplaces must check sellers' compliance information: [FiveX](https://fivex.com/nl/blog/gtm-gpsr-explained-chinese-sellers-manufacturers/)
- The Commission published GPSR guidelines on 19 Nov 2025 (C(2025) 7699). Chinese-origin products made up about 43% of 2025 alerts (secondary source): [ExamineChina](https://www.examinechina.com/?p=9850)

### Inferences
- **Landed-cost rule of thumb for China-direct single-item orders (estimate):** ≈ 1.2 × (AliExpress item + shipping + €3 duty), plus any EU handling fee. The 1.2 factor is the 20% import VAT the platform collects at checkout. Under the VAT franchise it **cannot be recovered**. I assume the VAT base includes duty (standard EU practice, not verified in these sources). The €3 alone adds about €3.60 per order. It hurts most on items costing €1–5 (lick mat, roller, paw cleaner).
- Shipping several units of the same product in one parcel probably pays the €3 once (one tariff line, per the Option Finance reading). Bundles therefore spread the duty. This needs confirming with DSers or the supplier.
- EU-warehouse stock avoids the per-parcel €3 because import happens in bulk upstream. This is the main structural reason to prefer EU stock in H2 2026, besides delivery time.
- For any electrical item, the micro-entrepreneur will likely have to register as the "producer" for DEEE (and batteries, if any) and pay eco-contributions. It must also hold GPSR, CE and RoHS documentation and print its name and address on the product or packaging. That is a material fixed-admin burden, so fountains are penalised on the regulatory criterion. Packaging REP applies to **every** candidate.

### Gaps
- Eco-contribution amounts for a small EEE item such as a pet fountain, and which eco-organisms accept micro-sellers. Not searched.
- The handling-fee amount and whether it has started.
- Whether textile car covers fall under the TLC or furniture REP streams. Not checked.

## 3. Candidate 1: cat water fountain (wired) + replacement filters ("fontaine à eau chat")

### Takeaway
An electric recirculating fountain (2–3 L, USB pump, filter cartridge) for cat owners worried about hydration, aimed at indoor and multi-cat homes and at heatwaves. Demand is the strongest of the set and filters give real repeat purchases. But mass retail has commoditised the base product: Action sells one at €9.99 (July 2026) and zooplus has its own Zen range. Add the electrical-compliance burden and this is a weak standalone bet unless positioned premium.

**Regulatory:** electrical and electronic equipment, so DEEE REP, IDU, GPSR and CE/RoHS apply (Section 2).

### Inferences
- **Landed cost (estimate):** assuming item + shipping of €6–12 from China, landed ≈ €11–18 per unit. Filter 6–12-packs are probably €1–3 landed (my estimate), which makes them the margin engine.
- **Pricing room:** Action (€9.99) and UAMLY on zooplus (€8.29) anchor generic wired fountains very low. A DTC store would need stainless or ceramic design plus a filter plan to sell at €24.90–34.90.
- **Typical buyer:** owners of indoor cats, multi-cat households and urban flat dwellers, often prompted by vet advice on hydration or by a heatwave (the Action launch was framed around a "canicule"). This is inference.
- **Differentiation:** filter subscription ("abonnement filtres" every 2 months); stainless or ceramic design; certified quiet pump; French customer service and warranty; bundles with a placemat. All are easy to copy except the subscription relationship.
- **Physical (estimate):** boxed about 20×20×20 cm, 0.8–1.5 kg. Pump failure or noise is the main return risk. Ceramic versions are fragile.
