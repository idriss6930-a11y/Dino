## 1. Micro-entreprise (EI, BIC "vente de marchandises"): 2026 social rate, CFP, versement libératoire, turnover ceiling, CFE

### Takeaway
- **Charges on turnover.** A micro-entrepreneur selling goods pays 12.3% social contributions plus 0.1% CFP on cash-in turnover (about 12.4%). With the optional 1% versement libératoire, the total is about 13.4%.
- **Ceiling.** The micro ceiling for sales is €203,100 per year for 2026–2028.
- **CFE.** CFE is nil in the creation year and halved the following year. No minimum CFE is due when turnover in year N-2 is at most €5,000.

### Cited Findings
- **Social contributions, sales of goods (BIC), from 1 Jan 2026: 12.3% of turnover.**
  - Sources: [Swim Legal, taux 2026](https://www.swim.legal/blog/taux-autoentrepreneur-2026-cotisations-impots); [Swim Legal, charges 2026](https://www.swim.legal/blog/charges-micro-entreprise-2026-taux-urssaf-calcul-obligations-d9294).
  - For dropshipping specifically: "12,3 % du chiffre d'affaires brut pour une activité commerciale de dropshipping" ([Join Jump](https://www.join-jump.com/guide/dropshipping-auto-entrepreneur)) [SECONDARY/COMMERCIAL].
  - An older guide still quotes 12.8% ([Rocket Lawyer](https://www.rocketlawyer.com/fr/fr/entreprise/creation-d-entreprise/guide-juridique/auto-entrepreneur-passage-en-sasu)); this is outdated.
- **CFP (contribution à la formation professionnelle): 0.1% of turnover for commerçants.**
  - Comparison: 0.3% for artisans, 0.2% for professions libérales. URSSAF collects it together with the contributions.
  - Sources: [Finalib 2026](https://finalib.fr/blog/retraite-prevoyance/micro-entrepreneur-cotisations-sociales-2026); [Ville de Versailles, "Présentation régime AE en 2026" (PDF)](https://www.versailles.fr/fileadmin/www.versailles.fr/MEDIA/emploi/Entreprendre/Ateliers/Presentation_regime_AE_en_2026.pdf) [SECONDARY].
  - **Conflict:** [Portail Auto-Entrepreneur](https://www.portail-autoentrepreneur.fr/academie/statut-auto-entrepreneur/cotisations) [COMMERCIAL] reportedly shows 0.20% for sales "au 1er juillet 2026", with an inconsistent 12.4% total. No other source corroborates it.
- **Versement libératoire (VL): 1% of turnover for sales of goods.** Combined with social contributions this gives 13.3% before CFP.
  - Sources: [Wisestart, taux VL 2026](https://wisestart.fr/guides/fiscalite/taux-versement-liberatoire-2026/) [SECONDARY]; official explainer [impots.gouv.fr – Le versement libératoire](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/professionnel/le-versement-liberatoire) [OFFICIAL, snippet only].
- **VL eligibility for 2026: household reference income (RFR) of 2024 at most €29,315 per part.**
  - By household size: €43,972 for 1.5 parts, €58,630 for 2 parts, €73,287 for 2.5 parts. The cap rises by 50% per extra half-part.
  - For 2027, one source gives €29,579 per part, based on 2025 RFR.
  - Pages quoting €27,478 or €28,797 refer to earlier years.
  - Sources: [La Micro by Flo](https://lamicrobyflo.fr/verifie-cette-option-avant-le-1er-octobre/); [La Micro by Flo simulator](https://lamicrobyflo.fr/outils/simulateur-versement-liberatoire/) [SECONDARY].
  - The same source treats "before 1 October" as the deadline to opt in for the following year.
- **Micro ceiling for 2026–2028.**
  - €203,100 HT for sales of goods and accommodation (previously €188,700).
  - €83,600 for services (previously €77,700).
  - Mixed activity: total turnover at most €203,100, of which services at most €83,600.
  - Pro rata in the creation year. Example: a start on 1 April 2026 gives 203,100 × 275/365 = €153,074.
  - Ceilings apply to cash-in turnover excluding VAT. Entreprendre.Service-Public confirmed the revaluation on 26 Feb 2026.
  - Sources: [Propulse by CA](https://propulsebyca.fr/actualites/nouveaux-plafonds-chiffre-affaires-2026-micro-entreprise); [CCI Paris Île-de-France](https://www.entreprises.cci-paris-idf.fr/actualites/micro-entrepreneur-revalorisation-des-seuils-pour-2026-2028); [TPE Actu, 25 Feb 2026](https://tpeactu.fr/2026/02/25/micro-entrepreneurs-plafonds-chiffre-affaires-2026/); [Legifiscal](https://www.legifiscal.fr/actualites-fiscales/4436-nouveaux-seuils-micro-entreprises-annee-2026.html) [SECONDARY].
- **CFE.**
  - Creation year: total and automatic exemption. The initial return 1447-C-SD is due before 31 December of that year.
  - First taxable year: 50% reduction of the tax base.
  - No minimum CFE when turnover of **N-2 is at most €5,000**. CFE 2026 therefore looks at 2024 turnover.
  - The minimum base depends on the commune's vote and on the N-2 turnover band. Quoted ranges differ: €237–7,933 in one source, €247–7,669 in another.
  - Sources: [LeFreelance, CFE 2026](https://www.lefreelance.fr/articles/cfe-auto-entrepreneur/); [LegalPlace](https://www.legalplace.fr/guides/cfe-micro-entreprise-domicile/); [Moicombien barème 2026](https://moicombien.fr/simulateur/cfe-auto-entrepreneur) [SECONDARY].

### Inferences
- **Proportional levy on every euro collected:** 12.4% without VL, 13.4% with VL.
  - Per €59 order: €7.32 / €7.91. Per €99 two-pack: €12.28 / €13.27.
  - If the 0.2% CFP figure were right, add €0.06 or €0.10. This is immaterial.
- **The levy is on turnover, not profit.** An order whose contribution margin is below about 13% loses money after URSSAF. The margin here means what remains after COGS, ads (including self-assessed VAT, see §3) and fees.
- **The ceiling does not bind.** The €203,100 micro ceiling is not the constraint; the €85,000 VAT franchise threshold (§2) binds much earlier.
- **Choosing the VL** (this relies on the 71% abatement, which is unverified; see Gaps):
  - Without VL, taxable income is about 29% of turnover, taxed at the household's marginal rate. At an 11% marginal rate that is about 3.2% of turnover, against 1% with VL.
  - VL is therefore cheaper for any taxable household under the RFR cap, and pointless for a non-taxable household.
  - As of 6 Nov 2026, the window to opt in for 2027 has closed (deadline about 30 Sept 2026). The next decision concerns 2028.

### Gaps
- **URSSAF page not opened.** autoentrepreneur.urssaf.fr was unreachable. Confirm there both the CFP (0.1% vs 0.2%) and that the 12.3% rate did not change in 2026.
- **[UNVERIFIED] background:**
  - The micro-BIC flat abatement for sales is 71% (art. 50-0 CGI, minimum €305).
  - TFCCI (CCI chamber fee) is about 0.015% of turnover for sales and 0.044% for commercial services. One search summary instead reported 0.044% "for commerçants".
  - ACRE terms in 2026: reported changes were not checked.
- **Shipping fees and refunds.** How shipping billed to customers and refunds enter declared turnover was not checked. [UNVERIFIED] Shipping billed to customers counts as turnover.
- **Limoges CFE.** The commune's CFE rate and minimum base were not found.

## 2. VAT franchise (art. 293 B CGI): 2026 thresholds, fate of the 2025 €25,000 reform, crossing the threshold mid-year

### Takeaway
- **2026 thresholds.** For sales of goods: €85,000 (base) and €93,500 (majoré). For services: €37,500 and €41,250.
- **The €25,000 reform never applied.** The single €25,000 threshold voted in the 2025 Finance Law was suspended until 31 Dec 2025, then repealed by loi n° 2025-1044 du 3 novembre 2025.
- **Crossing rules since 2025.**
  - Exceeding €93,500 ends the franchise on the day of crossing.
  - Exceeding €85,000 without reaching €93,500 ends it on 1 January of the following year. The former extra tolerance year is gone.

### Cited Findings
- **The 2025 reform.** The 2025 Finance Law created a single threshold of **€25,000 (majoré €27,500)**, due from 1 March 2025 ([Lextenso brève](https://www.labase-lextenso.fr/breves/franchise-en-base-de-tva-suppression-du-seuil-unique-d-exoneration-BREVEBO141)) [SECONDARY].
  - Its entry into force was **suspended until 31 Dec 2025**: [GHR](https://www.ghr.fr/reglementation-fiscalite/la-tva/suspension-jusqu-au-31-decembre-2025-de-l-abaissement-des-seuils-de-la); [BOFiP rescrit BOI-RES-TVA-000198](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/14620-PGP.html/identifiant%3DBOI-RES-TVA-000198-20250528) [OFFICIAL, snippet].
- **Repeal.** **Loi n° 2025-1044 du 3 novembre 2025** definitively removed the single threshold and kept the activity-specific thresholds in force at 1 Jan 2025.
  - Sources: [Lextenso](https://www.labase-lextenso.fr/breves/franchise-en-base-de-tva-suppression-du-seuil-unique-d-exoneration-BREVEBO141); [Bpifrance Création](https://bpifrance-creation.fr/entrepreneur/actualites/franchise-tva-reforme-annulee); [GHR](https://www.ghr.fr/reglementation-fiscalite/la-tva/suppression-de-la-reforme-de-la-franchise-en-base-de-tva) [SECONDARY].
  - One site gives "4 November 2025", probably the publication date in the Journal officiel.
- **2026 thresholds: €85,000 / €93,500 for sales of goods; €37,500 / €41,250 for services.**
  - Sources: [Abby](https://abby.fr/guide/fiscalite/seuil-franchise-tva); [La Micro by Flo](https://lamicrobyflo.fr/franchise-tva-seuils/); [Propulse by CA](https://propulsebyca.fr/actualites/nouveaux-plafonds-chiffre-affaires-2026-micro-entreprise) [SECONDARY].
  - Some 2026 blogs still wrongly say €25,000 applies (e.g., [Finactuel](https://finactuel.fr/micro-entreprise-vs-sasu-comparatif-2026/)). This is an error.
- **Crossing the majoré threshold during the year.** Since 1 Jan 2025 the franchise stops for operations carried out **from the date of crossing**, no longer from the first day of that month ([BOFiP BOI-RES-TVA-000198](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/14620-PGP.html/identifiant%3DBOI-RES-TVA-000198-20250528)) [OFFICIAL, snippet].
  - Every invoice issued from that day must carry VAT ([Copeps](https://copeps.fr/actualites/effet-mur-de-tva/)) [COMMERCIAL].
- **Crossing only the base threshold.** The franchise is lost on 1 January of the following year, and the former extra "tolerance year" no longer exists ([Copeps](https://copeps.fr/actualites/effet-mur-de-tva/)) [COMMERCIAL].
- **Invoice wording.** While under the franchise, invoices carry "TVA non applicable, art. 293 B du CGI" (not "autoliquidation") ([Abby](https://abby.fr/guide/fiscalite/mention-autoliquidation-tva-facture)) [COMMERCIAL].

### Inferences
- **Threshold in orders.** €85,000 ≈ 1,441 single-pillow orders at €59, or about 859 two-packs at €99, per calendar year.
- **Losing the franchise** turns €59 TTC into €49.17 HT, a 16.7% revenue cut, unless prices rise.
  - Part of this comes back as recoverable input VAT. Ads at 35–40% of revenue give back about 7–8% of revenue (self-assessed VAT, §3).
  - A further ~5% is recoverable on goods, but only if the supplier issues a proper VAT invoice, which is rare on AliExpress.
  - Net, the franchise is worth roughly 4–9 points of revenue for a high-ad-spend B2C store. Plan pricing before crossing €85k.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** The franchise is open to any taxable person established in France under the threshold, including an EURL or SASU. This matters for §4.

### Gaps
- **Primary texts not read.** Art. 293 B CGI and BOFiP BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-10-10 were not read directly.
- **Creation year.** Whether thresholds are pro-rated in a creation year was not verified.
- **PLF 2026 proposal.** A search summary mentioned that the PLF 2026 proposed a specific €25,000 threshold for building works. Final adoption was not verified, and it does not concern sales of goods.
- **2026 Finance Law.** No evidence was found that loi n° 2026-103 du 19 février 2026 changed the goods thresholds; 2026 sources describe them as unchanged.

## 3. Reverse charge (autoliquidation) on ads (Meta/Google/TikTok), Shopify and apps; intra-EU VAT number

### Takeaway
- **Yes, reverse charge applies.** A franchised micro-entrepreneur is still a taxable person. B2B services bought from Meta Platforms Ireland, Google Ireland, TikTok's EU entity, Shopify International (Ireland) or non-EU SaaS are taxable in France.
- **What the buyer must do.** Self-assess 20% French VAT (arts. 259-1° and 283-2 CGI), report it on a CA3 return and pay it with no deduction. Ad and SaaS spend therefore costs 1.20×.
- **VAT number.** An intra-EU VAT number is required (art. 286 ter CGI).

### Cited Findings
- **Scope of the reverse charge.** It covers services from EU **and non-EU** providers and applies **even under the franchise** ([LeFreelance, autoliquidation logiciels étrangers (2026)](https://lefreelance.fr/articles/autoliquidation-tva-logiciels-etrangers/)) [SECONDARY].
- **How Meta and Shopify invoice.** Facebook/Instagram ads are invoiced by **Meta Platforms Ireland** and the Shopify subscription by **Shopify International (Ireland)**, without VAT and with a reverse-charge mention.
  - The buyer computes French VAT and declares it on the **CA3** return. One source says a CA3 is filed for each month in which such an invoice was received.
  - Sources: [Sedomicilier, autoliquidation TVA auto-entrepreneur (2026)](https://en.sedomicilier.fr/articles/autoliquidation-tva-auto-entrepreneur); [Bizyness](https://www.bizyness.fr/blog/declaration-tva-auto-entrepreneur) [SECONDARY].
- **No deduction.** A franchisé **cannot deduct** the self-assessed VAT, so the 20% is a cost ([Kohen Avocats (2026)](https://kohenavocats.com/tva-intracommunautaire-auto-entrepreneur-acheter-vendre-ue-autoliquidation-preuves-2026/)) [SECONDARY, law firm].
- **VAT number (art. 286 ter CGI).** The article requires identification of a taxable person who receives services on which it owes VAT in France.
  - Another firm says the number is needed "dès le premier euro" and cites "286 ter, 5°". The paragraph number varies between sources.
  - Sources: [Kohen Avocats](https://kohenavocats.com/tva-intracommunautaire-auto-entrepreneur-acheter-vendre-ue-autoliquidation-preuves-2026/); [Squair Law](https://www.squairlaw.com/fr/blog/auto-entrepreneur-et-tva-guide-complet-2026) [SECONDARY].
  - The number is requested through the online professional space ([Calculer-TVA](https://www.calculer-tva.com/guides/tva-auto-entrepreneur/)) [COMMERCIAL].
- **Google.** For business addresses in the EU outside Ireland, Google does not add VAT to Google Workspace charges ([Google Workspace Admin Help](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/billing/taxes-in-your-region?hl=fr)) [OFFICIAL platform doc].
- **An apparent contradiction.** [Legalstart](https://www.legalstart.fr/fiches-pratiques/fiscalite-des-entreprises/autoliquidation-tva/) says auto-entrepreneurs are "generally not concerned" by reverse charge because of the franchise. Interpretation: this refers to their sales, not their purchases.

### Inferences
- **Cash cost.** For a franchisé, the cash cost of ads is HT spend × 1.20 (a CPA of €20 HT costs €24). The same applies to the Shopify plan, Shopify-billed apps and non-EU SaaS such as DSers.
- **Compliance path:**
  1. Obtain the French VAT number.
  2. Enter it in the Meta, Google, TikTok and Shopify billing settings, so that invoices are issued HT with reverse charge.
  3. File a CA3 for every period with such purchases.
  4. Pay the VAT.
- **If no VAT number is entered,** platforms may charge VAT themselves as they would for a consumer [UNVERIFIED]. The cash cost is similar, but the art. 283-2 obligation is formally unmet.
- **Payment fees.** Payment-processing fees (Shopify Payments, PayPal, Klarna) are normally VAT-exempt financial services, so nothing needs to be self-assessed [UNVERIFIED].
- **Purchases of goods.** With a French VAT number, buying goods located in France from a non-established seller could also make the buyer liable for VAT (art. 283-1, 2nd paragraph, CGI) [UNVERIFIED]. In practice AliExpress charges VAT at checkout (§6). Ask an accountant.

### Gaps
- **BOFiP not read.** No BOFiP page on art. 283-2 or 286 ter could be opened.
- **Filing and penalties.** CA3 periodicity for a franchisé with only reverse-charge operations is unclear. One commercial page claims a 5% penalty on omitted VAT; this is unverified.
- **Invoicing entities.** TikTok's invoicing entity and VAT practice, and DSers' invoicing entity, were not checked.
- **Shopify apps.** Whether third-party apps are invoiced by Shopify International was not checked. [UNVERIFIED] App charges appear on the Shopify bill.

## 4. When does an EURL/SASU at corporate tax (IS) beat micro for a high-ad-spend, low-net-margin dropshipper?

### Takeaway
- **Structural difference.** In micro, URSSAF (12.4–13.4%) is charged on turnover and ads/COGS are not deductible. In a company, only profit is taxed (IS), and ads and purchases are deductible.
- **Break-even.** With an operating margin below about 33%, typical for high-ad-spend dropshipping, a company at IS overtakes micro once turnover passes a low break-even:
  - about €19k at a 10% margin;
  - about €34k at 20%;
  - about €55k at 25%.
- **Assumptions behind the model:** IS at 15%, a 30% flat tax on dividends and €3k per year of extra running costs. These could not be verified this session.

### Cited Findings
- **Deductibility.** In micro, real expenses (ads, purchases) are not deductible; a flat abatement applies instead, and contributions are due on cash-in turnover even with a loss. In a SASU, ads, purchases and director pay are deductible.
  - Sources: [Dougs](https://www.dougs.fr/blog/votre-micro-entreprise-de-dropshipping-sasu/); [Superindep (2026 simulator)](https://www.superindep.fr/blog/2025/limites-frais-micro-entreprise/); [LegalPlace](https://www.legalplace.fr/guides/regimes-sasu-micro-entreprise/) [SECONDARY].
- **SASU president's charges.** The president is assimilé salarié. Social contributions are about 60% of gross pay, but only on pay actually paid, not on turnover ([Rocket Lawyer](https://www.rocketlawyer.com/fr/fr/entreprise/creation-d-entreprise/guide-juridique/auto-entrepreneur-passage-en-sasu)) [SECONDARY].
- **Running costs and choice criteria.** A company adds management and accounting costs. The choice depends on forecast turnover, expense level and social-protection needs.
  - Sources: [Superindep](https://www.superindep.fr/blog/2025/limites-frais-micro-entreprise/); [Hayot Expertise (2026)](https://hayot-expertise.fr/blog/passer-auto-entrepreneur-sasu-regles-2026) [SECONDARY].

### Inferences
This is a model; assumptions are flagged.

- **Definitions and assumptions:**
  - m = operating margin before owner taxes = (revenue − COGS − ads incl. VAT − SaaS − fees) / revenue.
  - F = extra yearly company running costs (accounting, bank, legal formalities). ASSUMED F = €3,000.
  - IS at the reduced SME rate of 15% (profit up to €42,500) [UNVERIFIED].
  - Profit paid out as dividends under a 30% flat tax [UNVERIFIED]. It may be 31.4% from 2026 if the LFSS 2026 CSG increase applies to dividends.
  - Both structures stay under the VAT franchise.
- **Formulas:**
  - Micro net to owner (with VL) = CA × (m − 0.134).
  - SASU net to owner (no salary, all dividends) = (CA × m − F) × 0.85 × 0.70 = 0.595 × (CA × m − F).
  - Break-even turnover CA\* = 0.595 F / (0.134 − 0.405 m). This holds while m < 33.1%; above 33.1%, micro always wins.
- **Break-even with F = €3,000:**

  | Operating margin m | Break-even turnover CA\* |
  |---|---|
  | 5% | about €15.7k |
  | 10% | about €19.1k |
  | 15% | about €24.4k |
  | 20% | about €33.7k |
  | 25% | about €54.5k |
  | 30% | about €143k |

- **Worked example.** Turnover €60k; COGS 35%; ads 35% HT (42% including VAT); SaaS and fees 5%. Then m = 18%, and micro nets about €2.8k versus about €4.6k for a SASU.
- **Caveats:**
  - A SASU paying no salary pays no pension or health contributions; micro contributions do open rights.
  - EURL at IS with a TNS manager: TNS contributions apply to pay, and to dividends above 10% of capital, premiums and current account [UNVERIFIED].
  - IS rises to 25% above €42,500 of profit [UNVERIFIED].
  - A company has set-up and closing costs and bookkeeping obligations, and loses the simplicity of the VL.
  - Once turnover exceeds the VAT franchise (€85k–93.5k), VAT applies under either structure.
- **Practical reading.** At Nuitalys' scale, the answer depends on the true margin m.
  - If m is 15% or less and yearly turnover exceeds about €25k, a company is likely better.
  - If the business stays small or m exceeds 30%, micro remains better.
  - Recompute with real figures and an accountant.

### Gaps
- **[UNVERIFIED], not searched:** 2026 IS rates and SME conditions; the 2026 PFU rate; TNS contribution rates; market rates for accounting fees.

## 5. EU customs reform (end of the €150 duty exemption, €3 interim duty, €2 handling fee) and the French small-parcel tax

### Takeaway
- **The €150 customs-duty exemption ended on 1 July 2026.** Council Regulation (EU) 2026/382 (11 Feb 2026) applies a temporary flat €3 duty per "item" to consignments of €150 or less, from 1 July 2026 to 1 July 2028. An "item" is each product declared on a separate line, so the duty is charged per tariff line, not per parcel.
- **EU handling fee.** A separate EU fee of €2 per item on distance-sales imports applies from 1 Nov 2026, under Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/2152 according to secondary sources.
- **French tax suspended.** France's own €2-per-item tax (2026 Finance Law, applied from 1 March 2026) has been suspended since 1 July 2026.
- **EU warehouses.** Stock already held in an EU warehouse escapes these per-parcel charges.

### Cited Findings
- **Regulation (EU) 2026/382 (11 Feb 2026).** It removes duty relief on low-value imports and introduces a temporary **€3 duty per item on consignments of €150 or less**, applicable **from 1 July 2026 to 1 July 2028**. The end date is the planned launch of the EU Customs Data Hub.
  - Sources: [European Commission, Access2Markets](https://trade.ec.europa.eu/access-to-markets/en/news/eu-applies-eu3-customs-duty-item-low-value-e-commerce-consignments) [OFFICIAL, snippet]; [Discogs seller notice](https://www.discogs.com/about/news/eu-customs-duty-council-regulation-2026-382/); [Avalara](https://www.avalara.com/us/en/blog/eu-end-150-customs-duty-exemption-2026.html) [COMMERCIAL].
- **Definition of "item":** "'item' means each unit of a product that is declared on a separate line" ([European Commission, Access2Markets](https://trade.ec.europa.eu/access-to-markets/en/news/eu-applies-eu3-customs-duty-item-low-value-e-commerce-consignments)).
  - The duty is "charged per item based on tariff classification, not simply per parcel or per shipment" ([Gerlach](https://gerlach-customs.com/knowledge-base/eu-parcel-duty-150-exemption/)) [COMMERCIAL].
- **Conflicting political dates.** One source dates the Council decision 12 Dec 2025, another 13 Nov 2025 (search results including [Avalara](https://www.avalara.com/us/en/blog/eu-end-150-customs-duty-exemption-2026.html) and [Stickiply](https://stickiply.com/blogs/small-business-focus/new-eu-customs-duty-july-2026); which source said which is unclear).
  - Interpretation: these are probably two steps, an agreement in principle in November and the €3 deal in December.
- **EU handling fee.** Commission **Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/2152** sets the Union handling fee (art. 20(2) of **Regulation (EU) 2026/2108**) at **€2 per item**, applying **from 1 Nov 2026**.
  - It was reportedly published in the OJ on 22 Oct 2026, and reportedly also covers distance sales above €150.
  - A lower fee is planned for goods sold from customs warehouses; the amount is still to be set.
  - Sources: [Lookuptax](https://lookuptax.com/tax-changes/european-union/union-handling-fee-delegated-reg-2026-2152); [LHR Rechtsanwälte](https://www.lhr-law.de/en/magazine-en/competition-law-antitrust-law/eu-handling-fee-2-euro-online-sellers/); [Dimweigh](https://dimweigh.com/blog/eu-2-euro-handling-fee-november-2026); [Taxually](https://www.taxually.com/blog/eu-handling-fee-3-euro-customs-duty) [SECONDARY/COMMERCIAL].
  - **Conflicts:**
    - [Global Trade Group](https://globaltradegroup.pl/en/blog/a-customs-duty-of-3-per-parcel-and-a-fee-of-2-what-applies-and-from-when) said the act was adopted but not yet published as of 23 Sept.
    - The Commission's older page called the fee "under discussion" for end-2026.
    - One source limits the fee to sales to customers who are not VAT-registered.
- **French small-parcel tax: €2 per article** in consignments **under €150** from outside the EU.
  - Destinations covered: metropolitan France, Martinique, Guadeloupe, Réunion and Monaco ([info.gouv.fr](https://www.info.gouv.fr/actualite/taxe-sur-les-petits-colis-comment-ca-marche)) [OFFICIAL, snippet]; [Aide-sociale.fr](https://www.aide-sociale.fr/taxe-petit-colis/).
  - Legal basis: **loi n° 2026-103 du 19 février 2026** de finances pour 2026. Article numbering conflicts: art. 82 per [Aide-sociale.fr](https://www.aide-sociale.fr/taxe-petit-colis/), art. 84 per [BDO Avocats](https://www.bdoavocats.fr/fr-fr/blog/actualite-juridique-fiscale-et-sociale/taxe-sur-les-petits-colis-et-envois-de-faible-valeur-echeance-francaise-au-1er-mars-2026).
  - **In force from 1 March 2026:** [CCI Lyon Métropole](https://www.lyon-metropole.cci.fr/actualite/taxe-2-euros-sur-les-petits-colis-entree-en-vigueur-mars-2026); [Weblex](https://www.weblex.fr/weblex-actualite/taxe-sur-les-petits-colis-c-est-parti).
  - It was designed to last until the EU handling fee arrived, or until 31 Dec 2026 at the latest ([Assemblée nationale, question n° 642](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/q17/17-642QOSD.htm)) [OFFICIAL].
- **French tax suspended from 1 July 2026.** Government communiqué n° 850 of 30 June 2026 suspended it to avoid stacking with the €3 EU duty, which would have meant €5 per article category.
  - Customs estimated that about 90% of the traffic had been diverted since 1 March, leaving a yield of about €2.3M per month.
  - Sources: [Legifiscal](https://www.legifiscal.fr/actualites-fiscales/4550-taxe-petits-colis-france-suspend-prelevement-2-profit-nouveau-droit-douane-europeen.html); [Journal du Net](https://www.journaldunet.com/business/action-publique/1552043-le-gouvernement-francais-annonce-la-suspension-a-compter-du-1er-juillet-2026-de-la-taxe-de-2-euros-sur-les-petits-colis-instauree-en-mars/); [CNews, 30 Jun 2026](https://www.cnews.fr/france/2026-06-30/petits-colis-la-taxe-francaise-de-2-euros-suspendue-partir-de-ce-mercredi-1883877); [Kohen Avocats, 1 Jul 2026](https://kohenavocats.fr/2026/07/01/taxe-petits-colis-suspendue-1er-juillet-2026-droit-douane-europeen/); [Univers Colis](https://www.universcolis.fr/actualites/taxe-2-euros-petits-colis-2026) [SECONDARY].
  - One secondary source links a decree n° 2026-589 of 3 July 2026 to the date. This is unconfirmed.

### Inferences
- **China-direct parcel with one product type:** €3 duty plus €2 handling fee (since 1 Nov 2026) = €5 before VAT. Two different product lines give €10.
  - On a €10–15 AliExpress item this adds 33–50% to the landed cost before VAT. The 20% import VAT then applies on top; the VAT base normally includes duties [UNVERIFIED].
- **EU-warehouse stock.** Duties are paid upstream at bulk import at the normal tariff rate, with no per-parcel duty or fee at consumer level.
  - This gives EU-stocked products a structural cost and delivery-experience advantage since July 2026, reinforced in November 2026.
  - Nuitalys' pillow, held in a French warehouse, is unaffected at order level.
- **Who pays.** Legally, the declarant/importer. In IOSS flows, the platform or seller collects at checkout and the charge ends up in the price the dropshipper pays. In non-IOSS flows, the consumer is asked to pay at the door plus a carrier fee [partly UNVERIFIED].
- **Not repealed.** The French €2 tax is suspended, not repealed. Watch for reactivation if the EU regime changes; its legal end date was 31 Dec 2026 at the latest.

### Gaps
- **Texts not read.** The EUR-Lex texts (Reg. 2026/382, Reg. 2026/2108, Del. Reg. 2026/2152), the 30 June 2026 communiqué and decree 2026-589 were not read.
- **[UNVERIFIED] background.** The December 2025 Council deal reportedly framed the €3 duty as applying per item category in IOSS-declared parcels (about 93% of e-commerce flows). Check whether non-IOSS low-value parcels are treated the same way.

## 6. VAT on China-direct sales (≤ €150) to French consumers: IOSS mechanics, who is liable, risk of fees at delivery

### Takeaway
- **Low-value imports are not VAT-exempt.** 20% French VAT is due in one of two ways:
  - at checkout through an IOSS registration: the platform or seller collects it and the parcel clears without charges; or
  - at import: the consumer pays VAT plus a carrier fee at the door.
- **IOSS is optional.**
- **In a DSers→AliExpress flow,** AliExpress acts as deemed supplier and normally collects the VAT from the dropshipper at checkout. A franchisé cannot recover it.
- **A March 2026 BOFiP rescrit** clarifies the case where no IOSS is used.

### Cited Findings
- **IOSS is optional** according to French customs. A seller who does not use it leaves VAT to be collected at import ([Douane, flyer IOSS vendeur](https://moa.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2021-06/01/flyer_IOSS_Vendeur.pdf)) [OFFICIAL].
  - Without IOSS, goods worth up to €150 destined for France must be cleared in France (same douane material, per search summary).
- **Fees at delivery.** Without IOSS, the buyer generally pays VAT **plus clearance fees charged by the carrier** ([Stripe](https://stripe.com/fr/resources/more/ioss-and-eu-vat)) [COMMERCIAL].
- **BOFiP rescrit BOI-RES-TVA-000184 (4 March 2026):** "Ventes à distance de biens importés … lorsque le commerçant n'adhère pas au guichet unique IOSS".
  - **Consignments of €150 or less without IOSS:** clearance must take place in the Member State of final destination. The snippet contains "l'importation n'est pas située en France et n'y est pas taxable", but does not show whether that sentence concerns customers in another Member State.
  - **Consignments above €150:** the merchant owes the French import VAT, no other person can owe it, and it is deductible under the ordinary rules.
  - Sources: [BOFiP](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/14482-PGP.html/identifiant=BOI-RES-TVA-000184-20260304) [OFFICIAL, snippet]. Commentary: [Socic](https://www.socic.fr/ressources-comptabilite/articles/dropshipping-tva-sans-ioss-guichet-unique-regles-bofip-2026-seuil-150-e-importation-et-facturation); [SMV Conseil, 18 Mar 2026](https://www.smvconseil.fr/index.php/2026/03/18/dropshipping-et-tva-precisions-utiles/); [L'Officiel des métiers](https://www.lofficieldesmetiers.fr/dropshipping-hors-ioss-le-nouveau-cadre-de-la-tva-a-limportation/) [SECONDARY].
  - A law firm stresses that **€150 is not a VAT exemption threshold** ([Kohen Avocats, 25 Aug 2026](https://kohenavocats.fr/2026/08/25/societe-dubai-dropshipping-tva-colis-importes-ioss-france/)).
- **AliExpress's IOSS** reportedly applies only when the order is paid through AliExpress checkout, with the marketplace acting as deemed supplier ([Shopify Community thread](https://community.shopify.com/t/does-aliexpress-handle-ioss-and-vat-for-dropshipping-in-the-eu-and-usa/236782/2)) [WEAK].

### Inferences
- **DSers flow.** DSers places orders through AliExpress checkout. AliExpress, as deemed supplier, usually adds 20% VAT (and now the €3 duty) to the price the dropshipper pays, and declares the parcel under its own IOSS number.
  - For a franchisé this VAT is an unrecoverable cost; the consumer should pay nothing at delivery.
- **Residual risk of charges at the door.** The French consumer may be asked for VAT plus a handling fee if:
  - the supplier ships outside AliExpress logistics;
  - the declared value exceeds €150 (for example, multi-item orders); or
  - the carrier ignores the IOSS number.

  The result is refusals, chargebacks and complaints. Because the Shopify store is the seller, the consumer-law exposure sits with the store: the price must include all taxes and fees, and a "no customs fees" claim would be misleading [UNVERIFIED legal hook: L112-1 / L221-5 Code de la consommation].
- **Keep China-direct consignments at €150 or less.** Per the BOFiP rescrit, above €150 the seller (the store) owes French import VAT, which a franchisé would pay without being able to deduct it.
- **Nuitalys' current product (French warehouse).** There is no import per order. However, the AliExpress price for EU-located stock of a non-EU seller probably also includes French VAT collected by the marketplace [UNVERIFIED].

### Gaps
- **Rescrit text.** The full rescrit was not read.
- **Own IOSS.** Whether a French franchisé can or should register for IOSS itself was not established.
- **Duty and fee pass-through.** How AliExpress passes on the €3 duty and €2 fee in IOSS parcels was not established.
- **Unconfirmed claim.** One secondary claim that the 2024 Finance Law subjected dropshippers to marketplace-like VAT rules is unsourced and unconfirmed.

## 7. GPSR (Regulation (EU) 2023/988, applicable since 13 Dec 2024): obligations for an online seller or dropshipper; DGCCRF enforcement

### Takeaway
- **In scope.** A dropshipping store is an economic operator covered by the GPSR.
- **Mandatory online information.** Every online offer must show:
  - the manufacturer's name and postal and electronic address;
  - an EU responsible person, if the manufacturer is outside the EU;
  - product identifiers, including a picture;
  - warnings and safety information in French.
- **Own brand.** A store selling under its own brand (Nuitalys) is treated as the manufacturer.
- **Enforcement.** DGCCRF found breaches on more than half the dropshipping sites it checked, and enforcement is increasing.

### Cited Findings
- **Applicability.** The GPSR has applied since 13 Dec 2024 and requires an EU-established responsible operator for products sold from outside the EU ([Gazette du Palais/Lextenso](https://www.labase-lextenso.fr/gazette-du-palais/GPL458h0); [Victoris Avocat](https://www.victorisavocat.com/blog/dropshipping-depuis-etranger-clients-francais-griefs)) [SECONDARY] (per search summary).
- **Dropshipping is not exempt.** Not handling the product does not exempt the seller: the store is part of the supply chain and owns the display obligations toward consumers ([Easecert, GPSR pour dropshippers](https://easecert.com/fr/blogs/connaissances/conformite-gpsr-pour-dropshippers); [Droppery (2026)](https://droppery.io/who-is-legally-responsible-in-dropshipping/)) [COMMERCIAL].
- **Own brand = manufacturer.** Anyone placing a product on the market under its own name or trademark is the manufacturer and bears all its obligations ([WisePIM GPSR guide](https://wisepim.com/fr/guides/gpsr)) [COMMERCIAL].
- **Art. 19 (distance sales): information required on the product page:**
  - manufacturer name or trademark, postal address and electronic address;
  - the EU responsible person's details, if the manufacturer is outside the EU;
  - product identification, including an image;
  - warnings and safety information **in the language(s) of the Member State** where the product is offered.
  - Sources: [Easecert, Amazon/Etsy/Shopify](https://easecert.com/fr/blogs/connaissances/gpsr-compliance-for-amazon-etsy-and-shopify-sellers); [donneespersonnelles.fr](https://donneespersonnelles.fr/gpsr) [COMMERCIAL].
  - Marketplaces check these fields and may block listings that lack them (one of the guides returned: [Easecert](https://easecert.com/fr/blogs/connaissances/gpsr-compliance-for-amazon-etsy-and-shopify-sellers), [Xictron (2026)](https://www.xictron.com/en/blog/gpsr-product-safety-regulation-online-shop-2026/)) [COMMERCIAL].
- **Art. 16 (EU responsible operator).** A product may only be placed on the EU market if an economic operator established in the EU is responsible for it, either an importer or a dedicated representative. The designation must be accepted in writing, and many logistics providers refuse it.
  - Sources: [WisePIM](https://wisepim.com/fr/guides/gpsr); [GetTransport (2026)](https://blog.gettransport.com/fr/logistics-guide/gpsr-eu-product-safety-2026-non-eu-seller-guide/) [COMMERCIAL].
- **DGCCRF enforcement:**
  - **2022 dropshipping survey (published Nov 2023):** 215 sites checked, 116 with anomalies (over 50%); the survey was repeated in 2023 ([DGCCRF](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/dgccrf/dropshipping-des-manquements-dans-la-moitie-des-sites-controles)) [OFFICIAL].
  - **Parliamentary answers** announce that DGCCRF controls on platforms will be "tripled", with better coordination with customs and **1,500 laboratory analyses of marketplace products in 2026** ([Assemblée nationale written answers (17th legislature), e.g. QE 4644](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/questions/QANR5L17QE4644.pdf), [QE 6725](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/questions/QANR5L17QE6725.pdf)) [OFFICIAL]. The snippets do not show which answer gives which figure.
  - **Activity report:** **15,000 firms** received corrective or repressive follow-up, i.e. **25% of the firms checked** against 21% in 2023 ([DGCCRF Rapport d'activité 2025 (PDF)](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/files/files/directions_services/dgccrf/media-document/Rapport-activite-2025-DGCCRF_0.pdf)) [OFFICIAL].
  - A consultancy claims that hidden or misleading delivery times are the leading ground for sanctions in dropshipping ([Hayot Expertise](https://hayot-expertise.fr/blog/dgccrf-controles-dropshipping-ecommerce-2026-conformite)) [COMMERCIAL, unverified].
  - DREETS Hauts-de-France publishes a dropshipping guidance sheet ([DREETS HdF (PDF)](https://hauts-de-france.dreets.gouv.fr/sites/hauts-de-france.dreets.gouv.fr/IMG/pdf/dropshipping.pdf)) [OFFICIAL, not read].

### Inferences
- **Nuitalys has two options:**
  - **(a) Sell under the Nuitalys brand.** Nuitalys is then the GPSR manufacturer and, being in France, can itself act as the EU responsible operator. It must:
    - hold a risk analysis and technical documentation;
    - make sure the product or packaging carries Nuitalys' name, address and a type/batch identifier;
    - provide instructions and warnings in French;
    - run a complaints channel;
    - handle accident notification and recalls (details unverified; see Gaps).
  - **(b) Sell as a distributor showing the real Chinese manufacturer.** Nuitalys must then display that manufacturer plus an EU responsible person, which most AliExpress suppliers cannot provide.
  - Option (a) is usually the only workable one, but it concentrates liability on the micro-entrepreneur, including under product-liability law.
- **Practical minimum on the pillow page:**
  - a manufacturer block: Nuitalys EI, postal address, email;
  - identifiers: model name or reference, batch policy, photo;
  - materials and composition;
  - a French safety and care text derived from a risk analysis. Typical items for a foam pillow: not for infants, airing out initial odour, keep away from heat sources, how to wash the cover versus the foam core. These are examples, not legal requirements.

### Gaps
- **[UNVERIFIED]** Primary text to check: Regulation (EU) 2023/988, https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj
  - Manufacturer duties (art. 9): internal risk analysis; technical documentation kept 10 years; type, batch or serial number on the product; name and postal/electronic address on the product, packaging or accompanying document; French instructions; accessible complaints channels.
  - Distributor duties (art. 12): check labelling before sale; stop selling and inform if the product is dangerous.
  - Traceability (art. 18): identify suppliers and business customers for a multi-year period.
  - Accident notification through the **Safety Business Gateway** (art. 20).
  - Recalls and remedies (arts. 35–37).
  - Penalties set nationally (art. 44); French amounts not checked.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** The new Product Liability Directive (EU) 2024/2853 must be transposed by 9 Dec 2026. Own-brand sellers and importers are strictly liable; fulfilment providers and platforms are liable in the alternative.
- **Not searched:** Shopify's GPSR product fields; DGCCRF GPSR-specific actions and sanctions in 2025–2026.

## 8. French EPR/REP (loi AGEC): IDU, Citeo, which filière per category, who is the "producteur" in dropshipping, penalties, CGV

### Takeaway
- **Registration.** Every REP producer must register with the relevant eco-organism and obtain one IDU per filière (in place since 1 Jan 2022). The IDU must be shown in the CGV and on the site.
- **Filière per category:**
  - Pillows and duvets ("produits rembourrés de couchage"): furniture filière, Ecomaison (not Refashion).
  - Toys and non-thermal DIY/garden: Ecomaison.
  - Sports and leisure: Ecologic.
  - Electrical items, including battery toys: ecosystem or Ecologic.
  - Household packaging: Citeo or Léko. Citeo's flat fee was €80 HT in 2025 for fewer than 10,000 units.
- **Producer status.** No source settles the dropshipping case. A French store selling in its own name to French consumers should treat itself as the producer.

### Cited Findings
- **IDU.** Since **1 Jan 2022**, an IDU is assigned to each REP producer that meets its obligations ([Adelphe fiche réflexe IDU (PDF)](https://cdn.adelphe.fr/IDENTIFIANT_UNIQUE/ADELPHE_FICHE_REFLEXE_ID_UNIQUE_VF.pdf) [OFFICIAL eco-organism]; [GHR fiche mémo IDU (PDF)](https://www.ghr.fr/IMG/pdf/fiche_memo_-_idu_fr.pdf)).
  - The Ministry of Environment issues it on registration, with **one IDU per filière** ([Projet Celsius](https://projetcelsius.com/blog/identifiant-unique-rep/)) [COMMERCIAL].
- **Display.** The IDU must appear in the **CGV and on the website** ([La Lettre des Réseaux](https://www.lettredesreseaux.com/identifiant-unique-mention-obligatoire-dans-les-cgv.html)) [SECONDARY].
  - The seller of a REP product must give the buyer, on request, the producer's IDU ([Adelphe](https://cdn.adelphe.fr/IDENTIFIANT_UNIQUE/ADELPHE_FICHE_REFLEXE_ID_UNIQUE_VF.pdf); [GHR](https://www.ghr.fr/IMG/pdf/fiche_memo_-_idu_fr.pdf), per search summary).
- **Marketplaces** carry REP obligations for third-party sellers unless they hold proof that the seller complies (IDU) ([AGN Avocats](https://www.agn-avocats.fr/blog/droit-contrats-distribution/places-de-marche-et-filieres-rep-quelles-obligations/); [Ecologic FAQ IDU & marketplaces (PDF)](https://www.ecologic-france.com/images/medias/document/18204/faqidentifiant-unique-et-places-de-marche-v10.pdf)).
- **Penalty.** A fiche mémo mentions an **administrative fine of up to €30,000**; the snippet does not say for which breach ([GHR fiche mémo](https://www.ghr.fr/IMG/pdf/fiche_memo_-_idu_fr.pdf)).
- **Pillows and bedding → Ecomaison.** Ecomaison's furniture (Éléments d'ameublement) scope includes "produits rembourrés d'assise ou de couchage : couettes, oreillers, traversin".
  - Ecomaison covers furniture, bedding, textile decoration, building materials, DIY/garden articles and toys ([Ecomaison (PDF, 2025)](https://ecomaison.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/EA-DEA-Partenariat-reemploi-Elements-dAmeublement-aux-structures-de-lESS-–-2025.pdf); [Ecomaison (PDF, 2026)](https://ecomaison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2026_Ecomaison_Journee-Environnement-Matelas.pdf)) [OFFICIAL eco-organism].
  - The eco-participation on a padded bedding item is quoted at **€0.06–0.18** ([Quelmatelas](https://www.quelmatelas.fr/ou-jeter-couette-jeter-oreiller-recycler)) [SECONDARY, possibly dated].
  - No official Refashion source was found for stuffed pillows.
- **Toys → Ecomaison.** Ecomaison (then Eco-mobilier) was approved for toys in April 2022, together with DIY/garden ([Ecomaison press release (PDF)](https://ecomaison.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/CP-Eco-mobilier_agrement-bricolage-et-jardin-VF-1.pdf); [ESS France/Ecomaison toys booklet (PDF)](https://www.ess-france.org/system/files/inline-files/Livret%20REP%20et%20ESS_Ecomaison_Jouets_VF.pdf)).
  - **Battery or electric toys stay in the EEE filière (Ecologic, ecosystem)** ([same booklet](https://www.ess-france.org/system/files/inline-files/Livret%20REP%20et%20ESS_Ecomaison_Jouets_VF.pdf)).
- **Sports and leisure → Ecologic.** Bikes, balls, rollers and similar items have their own filière run by Ecologic, separate from toys ([ESS France/Ecomaison toys booklet](https://www.ess-france.org/system/files/inline-files/Livret%20REP%20et%20ESS_Ecomaison_Jouets_VF.pdf); [ESS France/Ecologic ASL booklet (PDF)](https://ess-france.org/system/files/inline-files/Livret%20REP%20et%20ESS_Ecologic_ASL_VF.pdf)).
  - Sports articles are excluded from Ecomaison's DIY/garden scope ([Ecomaison éco-modulations guide 2026 (PDF)](https://ecomaison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Guide-des-eco-modulations-2026_MAJ-mars-2026.pdf)).
- **DIY/garden.** Ecomaison covers the general case; **Ecologic covers thermal DIY/garden articles (ABJ Th) since 24 Feb 2022** ([ESS France/Ecologic ABJ Th booklet (PDF)](https://ess-france.org/system/files/inline-files/Livret%20REP%20et%20ESS_Ecologic_ABJ%20th_VF_1.pdf)).
- **Household packaging (Citeo).** Companies placing **fewer than 10,000 UVC per year** pay a **flat €80 HT per year**, with no other declaration (2025 tariff) ([Citeo tarif EM 2025 (PDF)](https://bo.citeo.com/sites/default/files/2025-04/20241219_MAJ_Tarif_EM_2025-FR.pdf)) [OFFICIAL eco-organism].
  - Later consultancy pages cite **€110 HT** for 2026/2027 ([EPR Representative](https://eprrepresentative.com/blog/bareme-emballages-menagers-2027-citeo-leko)) [COMMERCIAL]. The conflict is unresolved.
  - From 10,000 to 500,000 UVC, a simplified declaration by product family applies. A UVC is a consumer sales unit, so a 3-item basket counts as 3 UVC ([EPR Representative](https://eprrepresentative.com/blog/bareme-emballages-menagers-2027-citeo-leko); [Projet Celsius](https://projetcelsius.com/blog/citeo-ou-leko-choisir-eco-organisme.md)) [COMMERCIAL].
- **Foreign sellers.** A seller with no French establishment that places packaged products on the French market, including at a distance, must act through a **mandated representative (mandataire)** in France. A vendor guide cites art. R541-174.
  - A vendor states this was made binding and subrogatory by **loi n° 2026-602 of 8 July 2026** (effective 10 July 2026). This is unconfirmed.
  - A supplier cannot act as the mandataire.
  - Sources: [Projet Celsius](https://projetcelsius.com/blog/mandataire-rep-emballages-france-vendeurs-etrangers/); [EPR Representative](https://eprrepresentative.com/blog/mandataire-rep-france-vendeurs-non-ue-guide) [COMMERCIAL].
- **PPWR.** Under the PPWR, the packaging producer is defined whatever the selling technique, distance contracts included ([PPWR Connect](https://ppwrconnect.com/fr/blog/ppwr-epr-distance-selling-which-member-state)) [COMMERCIAL].
  - PPWR art. 45 is said to apply since 12 Aug 2026 ([EPR Representative](https://eprrepresentative.com/blog/citeo-ou-leko-2027-choisir-selon-son-profil)) [COMMERCIAL].

### Inferences
- **"Producteur" in dropshipping.** No source addresses this directly. Nuitalys sells in its own name to French consumers, and the Chinese supplier has no French registration. The safest assumption is that Nuitalys is the producer for:
  - (a) the product filière: Ecomaison, furniture/padded bedding;
  - (b) household packaging: Citeo or Léko.

  The exception would be a supplier that proves its own French registration (IDU) covering these units. Shopify is not a marketplace, so no platform takes the obligation over.
- **Pillow with a removable cover.** The stuffed pillow falls under Ecomaison, and the cover is part of the same product. [UNVERIFIED] A pillowcase sold on its own would be "linge de maison" under Refashion/TLC.
- **Category-to-filière mapping for scoring:**

  | Category | Filière / eco-organism |
  |---|---|
  | Foam pillows, duvets | Ecomaison (EA) |
  | Bedding textiles sold alone (sheets, pillowcases, probably sleep masks) | Refashion (TLC) [UNVERIFIED] |
  | Small electricals | ecosystem or Ecologic (EEE), plus the batteries filière |
  | Toys | Ecomaison (electric toys → EEE) |
  | Sports and leisure | Ecologic (ASL) |
  | DIY/garden | Ecomaison (thermal → Ecologic) |
  | All categories | Packaging (Citeo/Léko) |

- **Cost versus risk.** The cost is small at this scale: cents per unit plus a packaging flat fee of about €80–110 per year. The real risk is failing to register: fines, marketplace delisting, and a missing IDU in the CGV.

### Gaps
- **Not verified:**
  - the exact wording of R541-173 and R541-174;
  - the sanction article (L541-9-4 / L541-9-5) and its amounts;
  - Ecomaison's 2026 eco-participation for pillows;
  - Refashion's scope for stuffed bedding;
  - whether loi 2026-602 exists as described.
- **[UNVERIFIED] background:**
  - The Triman logo and Info-tri sorting instructions are required on products and packaging subject to REP.
  - The "éco-participation" must be displayed separately for furniture and EEE.
  - The AGEC environmental-characteristics sheet duty applies only above turnover and volume thresholds that a micro-entrepreneur does not reach.

## 9. French consumer e-commerce law essentials

### Takeaway
- **The one point verified this session:** the hygiene/sealed-goods exception to the 14-day withdrawal right does **not** cover goods that can be cleaned and resold, such as a mattress whose film has been removed (CJEU slewo, C-681/17).
- **For pillows:** by the same reasoning, a foam pillow with a removable, washable cover almost certainly keeps the withdrawal right.
- Everything else in this section is background knowledge to be confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- **CJEU, 27 March 2019, slewo, C-681/17.** A mattress whose protective film the consumer removed after delivery is **not** excluded from the right of withdrawal.
  - The exception applies only where, once unsealed, the good can no longer be sold for health or hygiene reasons. A mattress can be cleaned or disinfected and resold, like clothing.
  - Sources: [CJEU press release (PDF)](https://curia.europa.eu/jcms/upload/docs/application/pdf/2019-03/cp190042fr.pdf) [OFFICIAL]; [INC](https://www.inc-conso.fr/content/achat-dun-matelas-sur-internet-descelle-apres-livraison-retractation-possible) [OFFICIAL consumer institute]; [Le Mag Juridique](https://www.lemag-juridique.com/nos-articles/categories/commercial-12183/articles/precisions-cjue-sur-notion-biens-scelles-lapplication-delai-retractation-239.htm).
  - The French transposition is art. L221-28 I 5° of the Code de la consommation (per summary of the [INC](https://www.inc-conso.fr/content/achat-dun-matelas-sur-internet-descelle-apres-livraison-retractation-possible) page).

### Inferences
- **Pillows, pillow covers, sleep masks.** Do not advertise a hygiene exclusion, and budget for returns (cleaning and resale, or write-off).
- **Mouth tape, adhesive nasal strips and other single-use items.** The exception is plausible only if each unit is genuinely sealed for hygiene and the consumer is told before purchase that opening it ends the right of withdrawal.
- **Reusable nasal dilators** can be disinfected, so the exception is arguable either way.
- **Personalised or print-on-demand items** fall under a separate exception for custom-made goods [UNVERIFIED: L221-28 3°].

### Gaps
All items below are **[UNVERIFIED]** (searches exhausted); check them on Légifrance and the DGCCRF site.
- **Pre-contract information (L111-1, L221-5):**
  - identity: EI name with the "EI" mention, address, email and phone, SIREN;
  - essential characteristics of the product;
  - total price including taxes and delivery; delivery date or deadline; payment terms;

  - legal guarantees in the standard wording (décret 2022-946);

- **Withdrawal:**

  - refund within 14 days, which may be held back until the goods or proof of shipment arrive (L221-24);
  - return costs fall on the consumer only if this was disclosed (L221-23).
- **Legal guarantee of conformity:**
  - 2 years from delivery, with defects presumed to exist for 24 months on new goods (L217-3 ff., L217-7, since 1 Jan 2022);

  - any announced reduction must show a reference price equal to the lowest price charged in the 30 days before the reduction (art. L112-1-1 Code de la consommation, in force since 28 May 2022, transposing Directive (EU) 2019/2161);
  - a Shopify "compare-at" strikethrough price counts as a reduction announcement;
  - fines for breaches of price-information rules were not checked.
- **Reviews and urgency:**
  - traders that publish reviews must say whether and how they are verified (L111-7-2);
  - fake reviews, fake "only X left" counters or countdowns, and falsely limited offers are blacklisted misleading practices (L121-4);
  - pre-ticked paid add-ons are banned (L121-17);
  - DSA art. 25 (dark patterns) targets online platforms rather than a single-brand store, but the misleading and aggressive-practice rules cover the same conduct.
- **Free shipping.** A "livraison gratuite" claim with a shipping charge at checkout is misleading. So is presenting shipping as "offered" when it is built into the price. The total price including delivery must be shown before ordering.
- **Delivery:**
  - deliver by the stated date, or within 30 days of the order if no date was given (L216-1);
  - the consumer can cancel after a formal notice if delivery is late (L216-6);
  - transport risk stays with the seller until receipt (L216-4), which matters for China-direct shipping.
- **Subscriptions.** "Résiliation en 3 clics" (L215-1-1, in force since 1 June 2023 under loi 2022-1158) applies to contracts that can be concluded online. It is only relevant if subscriptions are added.
- **BNPL and split payment:**
  - today, "paiement en 3/4 fois" over at most 3 months with negligible fees sits outside consumer-credit rules;
  - the new Consumer Credit Directive (EU) 2023/2225 applies from **20 Nov 2026** and brings BNPL into scope;
  - the French transposition text and its rules for merchant displays were not checked;
  - until verified: show BNPL only through the provider's compliant widgets, make no "free credit" claims and keep the provider's mandatory legal notices.
- **Environmental claims:**
  - Directive (EU) 2024/825 (ECGT) applies from 27 Sept 2026. It bans generic green claims, uncertified sustainability labels and neutrality claims based on offsetting;
  - France already bans "biodégradable" and "respectueux de l'environnement" claims on products (AGEC);
  - French transposition status not checked.
- **Cookies and marketing:**
  - the CNIL requires prior consent for Meta/TikTok pixels, with "refuse" as easy as "accept";
  - B2C email marketing needs opt-in, except the soft opt-in for existing customers.
- **European Accessibility Act.** E-commerce services have been covered since 28 June 2025, but microenterprises providing services (fewer than 10 staff and at most €2M turnover) are exempt.

## 10. Health and medical-device claim risk for sleep and wellness products; Meta and TikTok ad-policy constraints

### Takeaway
- **Not researched this session** (search budget exhausted).
- **[UNVERIFIED] governing principle.** A product becomes a medical device through its intended purpose as shown in its labelling and promotional or sales material (MDR 2017/745, art. 2(1) and 2(12)).
- **What that means in practice.** Claims to prevent, treat or relieve a disease or injury (cervical pain, sleep apnoea, scoliosis, hernia) trigger MDR conformity: CE marking (involving a notified body for most classes), a technical file, a person responsible for regulatory compliance (PRRC), and EUDAMED/UDI registration. AliExpress products lack these.
- **Comfort and wellness wording** keeps the product an ordinary consumer good.

### Cited Findings
- None obtained this session.

### Inferences
- **Low-risk wording:** "soutien de la nuque", "confort", "ergonomique", "mémoire de forme", "aide à trouver une position confortable".
- **High-risk wording** (likely a medical-device claim and/or a misleading health claim):
  - "soulage/guérit les cervicalgies, torticolis, migraines";
  - "orthopédique";
  - "recommandé par les kinés/ostéopathes" without proof;
  - "anti-apnée", "stoppe le ronflement";
  - "corrige la posture/scoliose";
  - "décompresse les hernies".
- **Reading by category:**
  - **Ergonomic or cervical pillows:** low risk if the wording is disciplined.
  - **Mouth tape and nasal dilators:** high risk. They are framed around snoring and apnoea, and many genuine products are CE-marked class I medical devices, so selling an uncertified clone with medical claims is a direct breach.
  - **Posture correctors and neck stretchers:** high risk, because their purpose is orthopaedic.
  - **Acupressure mats:** medium risk, because of pain-relief claims.

### Gaps
All items below are **[UNVERIFIED]**. Sources to check: the MDR text (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2017/745/oj), ANSM, DGCCRF, the Meta Transparency Center and TikTok's advertising policies.
- **Authorities.** MDR art. 7 bans misleading claims about devices. ANSM is the French market-surveillance authority for medical devices; DGCCRF treats health claims on non-medical consumer goods as misleading practices.
- **Meta Advertising Standards (personal health and appearance):**
  - no before/after images;
  - no content implying negative self-perception;
  - weight-loss and cosmetic ads restricted to people aged 18+;
  - medical devices may need certification or be limited by region.
- **TikTok:**
  - before/after imagery and unsubstantiated health claims are prohibited;
  - weight-management and medical-device ads are restricted.
- **Policies change often.** Re-check them before launching campaigns.
