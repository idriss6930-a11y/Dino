## 1. What trended in France in 2025–2026 according to trend sources?

### Takeaway
TikTok Shop France analytics (Lengow/Kalodata and Effinity) were the only rich, France-specific trend source with figures that I could reach for 2025–26:
- **Categories:** beauty leads, then womenswear, phones/electronics and household appliances.
- **Seasonal shifts:** Black Friday 2025 moved French demand to tech and appliances (Magcubic, Carrefour). January moved it to kitchen and routine products. Mid-2026 brought a home-improvement and cleaning surge (+202% in Q2; floor cleaners made up 13.3% of the August 2026 top-100 GMV).
- **Market structure:** sales are driven by affiliates (81.7% of top-50 shop GMV) and concentrated at €10–30 (44.8% of revenue).

Other sources were thinner:
- Google Year in Search 2025 France mainly shows food fads: Ciao Kombucha, Chocolat Dubaï, Matcha.
- Exploding Topics is weighted toward the US. It shows bag charms and magnetic shelves.
- I found no usable Amazon.fr "Mouvements" coverage and no AliExpress France-warehouse best-seller list.

### Cited Findings

**TikTok Shop France: scale and structure**
- TikTok Shop launched in France on 31 March 2025. Sales volumes grew 7× between April and September 2025 — [Headmind Partners](https://www.headmind.com/tiktok-shop-la-marketplace-qui-bouscule-le-e-commerce-traditionnel-en-france/)
- More than 16,000 merchants were active six months after launch — [E-commerce Nation](https://www.ecommerce-nation.fr/six-mois-apres-son-lancement-en-france-tiktok-shop-seduit-deja-plus-de-16-000-marchands/)
- By March 2026 France had 33,000+ sellers, about double October 2025 (≈16,500). They are part of 100,000+ sellers in Europe.
  - Sales rose 113% over six months, according to the head of TikTok Shop France.
  - Live shopping made up ≈20% of sales (an earlier article said 16%).
  - Growth was driven by Beauty, Fashion and FMCG ("Grande Consommation"). Home & Décor is the most popular category across Europe.
  - Sources: [FashionNetwork](https://fr.fashionnetwork.com/news/Tiktok-shop-a-franchi-la-barre-des-33-000-vendeurs-en-france,1815025.html); [Les Gens d'Internet, 13 Mar 2026](https://gensdinternet.fr/2026/03/13/plus-de-33-000-vendeurs-sont-presents-sur-tiktok-shop-en-france/)

  - Home Improvement grew +202% in France (DE +228%, ES +298%, IT +207%).
  - Category data covers 11 Apr–9 Jul 2026. Figures are Kalodata estimates, not official TikTok data.
  - Source: [Lengow, TikTok Shop Europe Q2 2026](https://blog.lengow.com/tiktok-shop-europe-q2-2026-e500m-across-four-markets)

### Inferences
- On TikTok Shop France, a candidate should meet three conditions:
  1. It can be demonstrated as a "visual transformation" (cleaning, whitening, beauty).
  2. It sells at roughly €10–40.
  3. It can carry a 15–20% affiliate commission on top of platform fees, since affiliates generate most GMV.
- France's seasonal rhythm in this data:
  - Black Friday: tech and appliances, deal-driven, with brand-direct sellers and Carrefour competing.
  - January: kitchen and routine products.
  - Q2–Q3 2026: home improvement and cleaning.
  - For a launch on 6 Nov 2026, the Black Friday window (27 Nov) favours tech and gifts. Personalized gifts are mainly a Christmas play.
- Food fads (matcha, Dubai chocolate, kombucha) are the strongest France-specific Google signals for 2025. Selling food is heavily regulated, so accessories such as matcha whisk kits are the low-regulation way to ride them.
- Kalodata estimates differ between providers. Effinity has ≈ €50.5M for one month in Oct–Nov 2025, while Lengow's Q2 2026 total works out to ≈ €44M/month despite the reported growth. Use these figures to rank categories, not to size the market.

## 3. Deep-dive: AI-styled personalized pet portraits (canvas or poster through EU print-on-demand)

### Takeaway
The French niche is real but crowded. I found at least six specialist or gift sites in one query, priced at about €16–25 for a mug or poster, €30–55 for a canvas, and €60 for premium canvases. Discounting is common.

- **Production:** EU print-on-demand is available through Gelato (local partners), Printful (Spain and Latvia) and Printify (UK, DE, CZ, PL, LV).
  - [EST] A 30×40 cm canvas costs about €22–29 landed in France, or €26–35 if 20% VAT applies and cannot be recovered.
  - [EST] A 30×40 cm poster costs about €10–15 landed.
- **AI generation:** $0.04–0.25 per image, which is cheap per order. The real operational risk is model churn: several image models are being retired around the Christmas 2026 peak.
- **Legal:** the 14-day withdrawal right probably does not apply (art. L221-28 3°), but buyers must be told before ordering. Raw AI images are probably not protected by copyright, so designs are easy to copy.
- **Christmas 2026:** realistic cut-offs for standard delivery are about 10–12 Dec for canvas and about 15–18 Dec for posters.
- **Score:** 66/100 (preliminary).

**Legal**
- **Copyright of AI images**
  - Under French law only a natural person can be an author, and the work must carry the imprint of the author's personality.
  - A purely autonomous AI creation fails the originality test, so a raw AI image is in principle unprotected.
  - Human retouching or recomposition can be protected for its original part.
  - An AI image that reproduces a protected work can be an infringement.
  - No Cour de cassation decision deals specifically with AI images. Some blogs cite cases I could not verify, so I ignored them.
  - Sources: [Riant Avocat](https://riant-avocat.fr/image-generee-ia-protection-droit-auteur/); [DRANE Bourgogne-Franche-Comté (Jul 2025)](https://drne.region-academique-bourgogne-franche-comte.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/IA_ET_DROIT-AUTEUR_VF27062025.pdf); [Créa-image](https://www.crea-image.net/images-generees-par-ia-et-droit-dauteur-le-guide-pour-un-usage-commercial/)
- **Withdrawal right**
  - Art. L221-28 3° excludes goods "confectionnés selon les spécifications du consommateur ou nettement personnalisés" — [Légifrance](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000044563170); [DGCCRF](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/dgccrf/les-fiches-pratiques/e-commerce-les-regles-entre-professionnels-et-consommateurs)
  - The exception is read strictly. A "personnalisable" product is not a "personnalisé" one, and choosing from standard catalog options is not enough — [Manoart](https://www.manoart.fr/blog/droit-retractation-sur-mesure/)
  - In a vehicle case (Cass., 17 Jan 2018), options with no specific work by the seller did not count as personalization — [Soulier Bunch](https://soulierbunch.com/remboursement-du-consommateur-apres-exercice-de-son-droit-de-retractation-et-notion-de-bien-personnalise/)
  - The seller must tell the buyer before the order that there is no withdrawal right (L221-5), and must be able to prove it — [Lettreclair](https://lettreclair.fr/blog/droit-retractation-14-jours-exceptions)

**AI pipeline and cost per order [EST]**
- **Flow:**
  1. The customer uploads a photo.
  2. Optional segmentation.

  6. Push to the POD platform's app or API.
- **Print resolution:**
  - 30×40 cm needs about 1772×2362 px at 150 dpi, or 3543×4724 px at 300 dpi (30 cm = 11.81 in; 40 cm = 15.75 in).
  - A "4K" output (about 4096 px on the long side, assumed) gives about 260 dpi on the 40 cm side.
  - A 1K output gives only about 65 dpi, so 1K previews need upscaling before printing (upscaler cost not sourced).
- **Scenario 1: generate only after purchase.**
  - Four 1K variants with Gemini 3.1 Flash Image cost 4 × $0.067 = $0.27, plus a 4K final at $0.151, for about **$0.42 per order**.
  - One re-roll brings it to about $0.70.
  - Using FLUX Kontext pro instead: 4 × $0.04 = $0.16, plus upscaling.
- **Scenario 2: free preview before purchase (the Milou.pet model).**
  - Each uploader costs 1–4 previews, or $0.04–0.27.
  - If 5–10% of uploaders buy (an assumption), the preview cost per order is **$0.40–5.40**, plus the final render.
  - Use watermarked, low-resolution previews and rate limits so people cannot use the site as a free generator.
- **Model churn is the bigger risk:** gpt-image-1 shut down on 23 Oct 2026, GPT Image 1.5 and 1 Mini retire on 1 Dec 2026 (at the start of the Christmas peak), and Gemini 2.5 Flash Image is deprecated. Build the pipeline so it can switch models, and test the fallback model before Black Friday.
- **Labour:** human QA takes [EST] 1–3 minutes per order. Offering one free revision before printing reduces disputes such as "it doesn't look like my dog".
